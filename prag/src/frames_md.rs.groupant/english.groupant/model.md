# English row assembly and lexical temporal routing

## forward

Skip Bare; Fact empty/be uses link.name; Say adds speech-act label. Negation and condition/irrealis/quote flags annotate verb. Route place TIME_NOUNS to when, otherwise goal() to other or ordinary place. Obliques prefer temporal classification before Goal/Result. Append NPs, words and attrs, then role provenance.

## behavior-complement

Universe: frame slices and proposed ten-column rows. Exclude a Bare row, an unsaid note for an empty role, a TIME_NOUNS place in where, or retaining when: prefix in the when cell. These describe general branch constraints, not merely test examples.

## reverse-reconstruction

A row constrains only displayed projections. Source/causal/quote/marker/dependency links remain unconstrained. A time string can originate in place, oblique, word or attr routes, so its antecedent role is ambiguous.

## invariant

Row order and original indices persist. In when, place precedes obliques, NPs, words and attrs. No visible frame yields the no-event note.

## cost

O(F+recursive phrase copying+output+sum oblique_count*how_role_count plus goal lookups). No throughput claim.

## time-partition

TIME_NOUNS membership routes a place to time regardless of case; obliques also accept TIME_PREPS. These lexical predicates are not a proof of temporal meaning. Native fixture transports in night, after meal, today.

## row-coordinate

Visible labels are original i+1, not visible rank. A leading Bare then Event yields row 2; missing coordinates reconstruct arbitrary compatible Bare frames.

## composition

Text helpers join Frame::goal, How::show and Tam::show. Native supplied Frame tests cover selected branches. External frame reader/parser is not run.
