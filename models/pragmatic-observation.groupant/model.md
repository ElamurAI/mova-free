# Pragmatic observation

## forward

A tagged family of agreement policy, frame invariant, role/context projection, lexical interpretation and irony observations. Five source leaves share supplied annotations, lexical snapshots, row IDs and policy configuration where actual APIs join; this ownership parent is not a mandatory sequential pipeline.

## invariant

Maintain original row/document/token coordinates and anchor alignment, voice-sensitive agreement precedence, diagnostic negative controls, cue-only irony exclusion and deterministic occurrence plurality ties. Diagnostic flags and lexical interpretations are hypotheses, not semantic truth.

## cost

Pay only reached child scans, map operations and text rendering. Chronological grouping and sorting, role extraction, frame analysis and lexical lookups have distinct costs; shared work must not be charged twice. No measured speed gain or general approximation guarantee.

## composition

Owned children are prag agree, invariants, lens, roles and irony. Actual frame construction depends on frames/Ctx, en annotations and global lexical knowledge outside this parent. Expression readings also join world domains across lexical-semantics. These external dependencies are explicit; registering this parent does not cover the entire frame/expression implementation, parser acquisition or full pragmatic pipeline. Same sentence identity and annotation order constrain joins.

## epoch-memory

Initial parent. Source review distinguishes occurrence plurality from majority truth, cue kinds from cue occurrences, and partial supplied-input transport from live context understanding. Twelve-source integration extends school/answer/state coverage independently of this parent.

## checks

Focused prag leaf tests and world/tests/groupant_wave12.rs supplied-input joins are the intended checks. The 27 focused pragmatic tests and ten root joins pass twice per profile; model.md#evidence binds their scoped execution. Child partial syntax is not a composed semantic AST.
