## forward

Snapshot bytes→store unpack/check→Graph→Kb indexed layers/assertions→query proofs→bridge/describe/pron observations. For query(c,p,L), A_l={ancestor seeds for p on l}; first nonempty visible layer l* decides, M removes dominated ancestor concepts, singleton values(M) gives Known with min(depth,index) witness, mixed gives Undetermined. HS tokens→Parsed→preference and candidate scoring s=WSD+state+chapter, then IsA/part/feature gates and derivatives.

## invariant

Shape-valid Graph is necessary but not semantic consistency: store does not verify key sorting, duplicate synset uniqueness, lexfile IDs, inverse CSR or DAG. Kb requires valid Cids/layers, maintained indices and acyclic layer ancestry; read-only query consumers preserve graph. Valid offsets/unit dimensions/finite Rat and bounded token levels are explicit at respective joins.

## cost

Store O(bytes+arrays), Kb BFS O(Va+Ea), property K candidate ancestry maps plus O(K²) specificity; query repeated traversal and bounded-depth broad parts. HS token scans may O(T²), mapping neighbour gates quadratic per heading; build retains all descriptions and writes sequentially. No measured speed claim.

## domain

Layer-chain visibility assumes leak_all_layers=false. Multi-sense ranking additionally assumes finite scores; executed fixtures use one sense.
