# Particles and syntax of Jewish Palestinian Aramaic

This file covers the small words of JPA, the clause patterns of its narrative and argument, and the Yerushalmi's own technical terms, which differ from the Bavli's ([../tmr/discourse-terms.md](../tmr/discourse-terms.md)). References: Dalman 1905 (particles, syntax); Sokoloff 2002b s.vv.; Fassberg 1991; Golomb 1985. Counts: occurrences of the string in the unpointed Guggenheimer Yerushalmi (CC BY), 2026-10-17; approximate where a string can occur inside other words.

## 1. ד־ and דִּי

JPA uses the prefix ד־ for the relative, the genitive and the complementiser, as all Late Aramaic does ([../arc/particles-syntax.md](../arc/particles-syntax.md) §1). Examples from y. Berakhot 2:8: דִּינֵיהּ דְּהַהוּא גַבְרָא "the sentence of that man" (genitive with anticipatory suffix); בַּר נַשׁ דְּאִימֵּיהּ מְבַסְּרָא לֵיהּ "a man whose mother despises him" (relative with a resumptive suffix); מִן הֵן דִּסְלִיקִית "from where I came up". In Neofiti the separate דִּי also occurs, as in Onkelos (Golomb 1985).

## 2. The object

| marker | where | example |
|---|---|---|
| **יָת** | Palestinian targums (Neofiti, Fragment targums), following the Hebrew אֶת | Fragment targum Gen 4:8: וְקָטֵל יָתֵיהּ "and killed him"; Exod 15:18: יַת נִיסַיָּיא "the miracles" |
| **ל־** | the Yerushalmi and midrash, with animate definite objects | |
| suffix on the verb | everywhere | חַמְתֵּיהּ "(he) saw him" (y. Berakhot 2:8); זְבַנּוֹן לֵיהּ "they bought for him" |
| none | indefinite objects | זְבַנּוֹן לֵיהּ חָדָא חֲמָר "they bought him a donkey" |

So the targums keep יָת, the Talmud does not; a classifier must not take יָת alone as a sign of `arc-x-targum` ([mova.md](mova.md) §3).

## 5. Negation and existence

| form | meaning | example |
|---|---|---|
| לָא | not | אָמְרִין לֵיהּ לָא "they said: no" |
| **לֵית** | there is not; with a pronoun: "not" | לֵית אַתְּ לָעֵי "you will not toil"; לֵית אַתְּ צְרִיךְ "you do not need" (y. Bava Metzia 2:5) |
| **אִית** | there is; with ל־: "have" | אִית לָךְ קוֹפָּד סַגִּי "you will have much meat" (y. Maaser Sheni 4:6) |
| **לֵית … אֶלָּא** | only | |
| לָא … וְלָא | neither … nor | |

The negated existential **לֵית** + pronoun + participle is a western way to negate the present tense ([verbs.md](verbs.md) §5). JBA uses לָא + participle and has לֵיכָּא for "there is not".

## 8. Narrative style

JPA narrative (the Yerushalmi stories, the midrash) is quick and paratactic, built from short clauses joined by וְ־, with dialogue introduced by **אֲמַר לֵיהּ / אֲמַר לוֹן / אָמְרִין לֵיהּ** (2 437 / 481 / 120 in the Yerushalmi). Typical features, all seen in the story of Simeon ben Shetaḥ (y. Bava Metzia 2:5, [texts.md](texts.md) §3):

- new participants introduced with **חַד** "one, a certain": חַד סִירְקַאי "a Saracen";
- **3 m. pl. perfects in ־וֹן**: אַזְלוֹן זְבַנּוֹן … אֲתוֹן "they went, bought …, came";
- direct speech without a verb of saying after the first exchange;
- the **participle** for planned or future action: וַאֲנָן זְבַנִּין לָךְ "and we will buy you";
- a **moral at the end**, often a saying of a rabbi or a proverb.

Word order is mostly verb-first in narrative (אַזְלוֹן זְבַנּוֹן לֵיהּ …), subject-first when a new topic is introduced (שִׁמְעוֹן בֶּן שֶׁטַח הֲוָה עֲסִיק …).
