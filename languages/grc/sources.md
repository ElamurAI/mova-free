# Sources

The manual is written in our own words. The works below are where its facts were checked. Public-domain grammars are the base. Smyth is cited by section and paraphrased from the Perseus text (CC BY-SA). The classical treebanks under non-commercial terms are counted and never quoted. Each work has a card in the Elamur Research library under its slug, and the chapters that cite it are listed after it.
