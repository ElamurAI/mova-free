# The verb in Jewish Babylonian Aramaic

The JBA verb keeps the Aramaic stems and conjugations ([../arc/verbs.md](../arc/verbs.md)) but changes three things that a reader notices on every page of the Bavli:

1. the **imperfect** takes the prefix **ל־** (and **נ־**) in the 3rd person, not י־: לֵימָא "let him say", נֵימָא "let us say / shall we say";
2. the **participle with an enclitic pronoun** is the present tense, often with the particle **קָא**: קָאָמַר "he is saying", אָמֵינָא "I say", אָמְרִינַן "we say";
3. the infinitives of the derived stems end in **־וּיֵי / ־יֵי**: לְאַפּוֹקֵי "to exclude", לְאַתּוֹיֵי "to include".

References: Margolis 1910 §§ 27–41 (stems, paradigms); Epstein 1960; Bar-Asher Siegal 2013 (the verb); Juusola 1999, *Linguistic Peculiarities in the Aramaic Magic Bowl Texts*; Wikipedia "Jewish Babylonian Aramaic" (tables, 2026-10-17). Vowels are reconstructions. Counts: unpointed tokens of the Wikisource Bavli (CC BY-SA, measurement only), 2026-10-17; they include homographs (אמרי is both "my sayings" and "they say").

Notes:

- **The causative is aphel**, as in the targums and JPA. JBA spells the doubled consonant of I-y/I-ʾ verbs with יי: אַיְיתֵי (from אתי "come", aphel "bring"; 173×).

In the incantation bowls the prefix is mostly י־, with נ־ in some bowls (Nos. 6, 13, 19, 25, 28); Montgomery notes no ל־ (Montgomery 1913, p. 30): the ל־ imperfect is a feature of the Talmud text, not of all Babylonian Jewish Aramaic.

**The l- prefix.** Where western Aramaic has י־ in the 3rd person, JBA has ל־ (with נ־ beside it in some texts and in the 1 pl.). The prefix is shared with Mandaic (ל־ and נ־) and parallels Syriac נ־ (Bar-Asher Siegal 2013; Kutscher 1971). The isolated לֶהֱוֵא of Biblical Aramaic ([../arc/verbs.md](../arc/verbs.md) §4.1) is sometimes cited as an early instance of the same process, though its explanation is disputed.

**Uses.** The JBA imperfect is mostly **modal**: wishes, commands, purpose and, above all, the **proposals of the argument**: לֵימָא "shall we say …?" introduces a suggestion to be tested; נֵימָא "let us say"; לִיתְנֵי "(the Mishnah) should have taught …!" ([discourse-terms.md](discourse-terms.md)). Plain future is often expressed by the participle.

### 5.2 The particle קָא

קָא (from קָאֵם "standing", Jastrow s.v.) before a participle marks the action as **in progress or in focus**; it is often written together with the verb (קָאָמַר, קָתָנֵי) and can follow ד־ (דְּקָאָמַר "who says"). Jastrow calls it "a particle of emphasis, mostly untranslatable". It also occurs with **perfects** in some manuscripts, and in a contracted form קְ־ / קַ־ (Bar-Asher Siegal 2013). Counts: קָא as a separate word 2 828×; fused forms many more.

```
Aramaic:   לָאו עֲלַי דִּידִי קָא סָמְכַתְּ
Translit.: lāw ʿălay dīḏī qā sāmḵat (reconstr.)
Gloss:     NEG.Q on-1SG mine DUR rely.PEAL.PTCP.ACT-2MS
English:   Is it not on me that you are relying?
Source:    b. Shabbat 31a (Wikisource Bavli, CC BY-SA; written לאו עלי דידי קא סמכת)
```

## 7. Object marking on the verb

The object is a suffix on the verb, or ל־ + noun, or **both** (prolepsis): the verb takes a suffix agreeing with a following ל־ object. This is the single most typical JBA clause pattern:

| sentence | translation | source |
|---|---|---|
| אַשְׁכְּחֵיהּ רַבִּי נָתָן לְאֵלִיָּהוּ | R. Nathan found Elijah | BM 59b |
| חַזְיֵיהּ לְהַהוּא גַּבְרָא | he saw a certain man | Taanit 23a |
| שַׁמְעִינְהוּ לְרַבָּנַן | he heard the rabbis | Taanit 23a |
| חֲזָא לַחֲמָרֵיהּ | he saw his she-ass | Taanit 23a |

For trees: the ל־ is `case` of an `obj`, and the suffix is a pronoun that **doubles** the object (UD: an `expl`-like or agreement feature; Mova should choose one convention and keep it, [mova.md](mova.md) §4).

## 8. Tense and aspect, in sum

| form | main use in the Bavli |
|---|---|
| perfect | narrative past; past reports of opinions (אֲמַר רַב "Rav said") |
| participle (± קָא, ± enclitic) | present; habitual; the reading of a source ("he means": קָאָמַר); general truths |
| הֲוָה + participle | past progressive and habitual |
| הֲוָה אָמֵינָא | counterfactual "I would have thought" |
| imperfect (ל־ / נ־) | modal: proposals (לֵימָא), wishes, purpose, commands |
| imperative | the moves of the argument: תָּא שְׁמַע, אֵימָא, תְּנֵי |
| ithpeel perfect | impersonal reports: אִיתְּמַר "it was said", אִיבַּעְיָא לְהוּ "they asked" |
