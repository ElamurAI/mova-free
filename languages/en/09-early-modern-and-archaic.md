# 09. Early Modern and archaic forms

Early Modern English (about 1500–1700) and the biblical register that continues it — the King James Bible (1611), the JPS 1917 Tanakh, Shakespeare, the old voice of 19th-century tales — have their own manual: [Early Modern English and the biblical register (`en-x-emode`)](../en-x-emode/README.md). It says what each feature is, how it differs from modern English, how Mova parses it today, where it fails (measured), and what to change.
