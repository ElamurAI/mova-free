## Part 1: sounds, spelling, forms and lexicon (chapters 00–19)

Every work cited in chapters 00–19, with its licence, its place in the research library and an open URL.

- **Library paths** are relative to the research library (`books/<letter>/<two letters>/<slug>/`, with the extracted text in `text-N.md` and page markers); raw downloads are named by their folder in the raw store (`en-gram-…`) or in `$MOVA_DATA/raw/`.
- **Licence** is the licence of the source. Public-domain grammars are used freely but paraphrased; CC BY-SA data (EWT, Wiktionary, PARSEME, STREUSLE, NGSL, wordfreq) is quoted little and never carried into the manual in bulk; NC data (GUM, LADEC, MorphoLex) is used for counts and tests only ([licence/DATA.md](../../licence/DATA.md)).
- Counts made for this manual are dated 2026-10-17.

## Part 2: syntax and pragmatics (chapters 20–38)

The works cited in chapters 20–38 of this manual, grouped by topic, with where each is used, and the key works the library still lacks (*Wanted*). Phonology, morphology and the lexicon (files 0x and 1x) keep their own sources.

### How sources are cited

| form | what it points to |
|---|---|
| `books/<a>/<ab>/<slug>` | a book in the project's research library: a card (`README.md`), often Mova's summary, and, where the licence allows, the text. "card" after a path means only the card and summary are there. |
| `papers/<a>/<ab>/…/<slug>` | a paper in the research library (cards are three or four levels deep). |
| `https://aclanthology.org/<id>` | ACL Anthology, open (CC BY 4.0 from 2016; CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 before). |
| `docs/papers/<topic>.md` | a topic guide of the research library (UD digests, QUD, narrative). |
| `../../<path>` | a file in this repository. |
| `$MOVA_DATA/...` | Mova's data directory (outside the repo): the parse database, the Torah trees, runs. |

Licences of the example data: UD English EWT is CC BY-SA 4.0 — quoted, never shipped as data; GUM is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — counts and very short fragments only; Mova's school lessons are ours; the tales and the JPS 1917 Torah are public domain ([../../licence/DATA.md](../../licence/DATA.md)). All counts are dated in the chapters (mostly 2026-10-17).
