# 05b. Verb paradigms: the strong verb in seven binyanim and the weak roots

This file holds the paradigm tables that go with [05-verbs.md](05-verbs.md), which explains the system (roots, binyanim, tenses, the imperative and its substitutes, spoken levelling). Section 1 lists the affixes common to all binyanim; section 2 gives a full paradigm of a strong root in each binyan; section 3 gives the key cells of every weak-root class, with the main colloquial variants. Index: [README.md](README.md). Neighbours: [04-nouns-adjectives-pronouns.md](04-nouns-adjectives-pronouns.md), [06-prepositions-particles.md](06-prepositions-particles.md), [07-syntax.md](07-syntax.md).

**How to read the tables.** Each cell gives the pointed (vocalised) normative form, then the transliteration of the normal Israeli pronunciation (chapter 02 conventions: stress marked only when not final; *kh* = /χ/; *'* = an actually pronounced glottal stop). The ordinary unpointed spelling in *ktiv male* is given in the first column of each binyan header and wherever it differs notably (*i* written with י in pi'el and nif'al future, *o/u* written with ו). Forms labelled **formal** are normative but rare in speech; forms labelled **spoken** are the usual colloquial forms (05-verbs.md § 10). The cells were written for this manual and checked against UniMorph Hebrew (`heb_voc`, from Wiktionary; Goldman, UniMorph 4.0) wherever UniMorph has the verb; disagreements are noted.

## 1. Affixes shared by all binyanim

The past and future affixes are the same in every binyan; only the stem changes.

### 1.1 Past (suffixes on the past stem)

| Person | Suffix | Example (pa'al כתב) |
|---|---|---|
| 1SG | ־תִּי *-ti* | כתבתי *katávti* |
| 2MS | ־תָּ *-ta* | כתבת *katávta* |
| 2FS | ־תְּ *-t* | כתבת *katavt* |
| 3MS | — | כתב *katav* |
| 3FS | ־ָה *-a* | כתבה *katva* |
| 1PL | ־נוּ *-nu* | כתבנו *katávnu* |
| 2MPL | ־תֶּם *-tem* | כתבתם *ktavtem* (spoken *katávtem*) |
| 2FPL | ־תֶּן *-ten* | כתבתן *ktavten* (spoken *katávten*) |
| 3PL | ־וּ *-u* | כתבו *katvu* |

Notes. 2MS and 2FS are spelled alike without points (כתבת); in speech they differ (*katávta* / *katavt*). The past has no gender distinction in the 1st person and in the 3PL. The suffixes are stressless except 3FS and 3PL, which take the stress in pa'al/nif'al/pi'el/pu'al/hitpa'el (*katva*, *katvu*) and leave it on the stem in hif'il (*hirgísha*, *hirgíshu*). When the root ends in ת or ד, the suffix *t* merges: *pakádti* (פקדתי) is regular, but *natáti* "I gave" from נ-ת-נ shows assimilation of *n* to *t*.

### 1.2 Future (prefixes and suffixes on the future stem)

| Person | Affix | Example (pa'al כתב) |
|---|---|---|
| 1SG | א־ *e-/a-* | אכתוב *ekhtov* (spoken *yikhtov*, *ikhtov*) |
| 2MS | ת־ *ti-/te-/ta-* | תכתוב *tikhtov* |
| 2FS | ת־…־י *-i* | תכתבי *tikhtevi* |
| 3MS | י־ *yi-/ye-/ya-* | יכתוב *yikhtov* |
| 3FS | ת־ | תכתוב *tikhtov* (= 2MS) |
| 1PL | נ־ *ni-/ne-/na-* | נכתוב *nikhtov* |
| 2PL | ת־…־וּ *-u* | תכתבו *tikhtevu* |
| 2FPL (formal) | ת־…־נָה *-na* | תכתובנה *tikhtóvna* |
| 3PL | י־…־וּ | יכתבו *yikhtevu* |
| 3FPL (formal) | ת־…־נָה | תכתובנה *tikhtóvna* |

The prefix vowel depends on the binyan: *i* in pa'al (strong), nif'al and hitpa'el; *e* (written ְ) in pi'el and pu'al; *a* in hif'il and in pa'al of some weak roots; *u* in huf'al.

### 1.3 Present (participle endings)

| | Endings | Example (pa'al) |
|---|---|---|
| MS | — | כותב *kotev* |
| FS | ־ֶת *-et* or ־ָה *-a* | כותבת *kotévet*; (hif'il) מרגישה *margisha* |
| MPL | ־ִים *-im* | כותבים *kotvim* |
| FPL | ־וֹת *-ot* | כותבות *kotvot* |

The FS ending is *-et* (unstressed, with a segolate stem: *kotévet*, *medabéret*, *mitlabéshet*) in pa'al, pi'el, pu'al, hitpa'el and most nif'al verbs, and stressed *-a* in hif'il (*margisha*), hollow pa'al (*kama*), final-weak verbs (*kona*) and some nif'al verbs.

### 1.4 Imperative

The imperative is the future 2nd person without the prefix: MS (no ending), FS *-i*, MPL *-u*, FPL *-na* (formal). Nif'al and hif'il restore a prefix *hi-* / *ha-*. Pu'al and huf'al have none.

## 2. The strong verb in each binyan

### 2.1 Pa'al — כ-ת-ב "write" (future in *o*)

| | Past | | Future | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1SG | כָּתַבְתִּי | *katávti* | אֶכְתֹּב | *ekhtov* |
| 2MS | כָּתַבְתָּ | *katávta* | תִּכְתֹּב | *tikhtov* |
| 2FS | כָּתַבְתְּ | *katavt* | תִּכְתְּבִי | *tikhtevi* |
| 3MS | כָּתַב | *katav* | יִכְתֹּב | *yikhtov* |
| 3FS | כָּתְבָה | *katva* | תִּכְתֹּב | *tikhtov* |
| 1PL | כָּתַבְנוּ | *katávnu* | נִכְתֹּב | *nikhtov* |
| 2MPL | כְּתַבְתֶּם | *ktavtem*, spoken *katávtem* | תִּכְתְּבוּ | *tikhtevu* |
| 2FPL | כְּתַבְתֶּן | *ktavten*, spoken *katávten* | תִּכְתֹּבְנָה | *tikhtóvna* (formal) |
| 3MPL | כָּתְבוּ | *katvu* | יִכְתְּבוּ | *yikhtevu* |
| 3FPL | כָּתְבוּ | *katvu* | תִּכְתֹּבְנָה | *tikhtóvna* (formal; spoken *yikhtevu*) |

| Present | | Imperative | | Non-finite | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MS כּוֹתֵב | *kotev* | MS כְּתֹב | *ktov* | INF לִכְתֹּב | *likhtov* |
| FS כּוֹתֶבֶת | *kotévet* | FS כִּתְבִי | *kitvi* | VN כְּתִיבָה | *ktiva* |
| MPL כּוֹתְבִים | *kotvim* | MPL כִּתְבוּ | *kitvu* | PASS.PTCP כָּתוּב | *katuv* |
| FPL כּוֹתְבוֹת | *kotvot* | FPL כְּתֹבְנָה | *któvna* (formal) | | |

Unpointed future spellings: אכתוב, תכתוב, יכתוב (ו for *o*); imperative כתוב.

Spirantisation: כ is [k] at the start of a word (*katav*) and [kh] after a vowel prefix (*yikhtov*, *likhtov*); ב is [v] throughout this paradigm, because it always follows a vowel or a shva that was historically vocal (*kitvi* goes back to \*ketubi).

**Pa'al with future in *a*.** Verbs whose 2nd or 3rd root consonant is a guttural, and some statives, take *a* in the future and imperative but keep *o* in the infinitive:

| | למד "learn" | שכב "lie down" | שמע "hear" |
|---|---|---|---|
| past 3MS | לָמַד *lamad* | שָׁכַב *shakhav* | שָׁמַע *shama* |
| future 3MS | יִלְמַד *yilmad* | יִשְׁכַּב *yishkav* | יִשְׁמַע *yishma* |
| imperative MS | לְמַד *lmad* | שְׁכַב *shkav* | שְׁמַע *shma* |
| infinitive | לִלְמֹד *lilmod* | לִשְׁכַּב *lishkav* | לִשְׁמֹעַ *lishmóa* |

| | Past | Present | Future | Imperative | INF | VN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MS / 3MS | טִלְפֵּן *tilfen* | מְטַלְפֵּן *metalfen* | יְטַלְפֵּן *yetalfen* | טַלְפֵּן *talfen* | לְטַלְפֵּן *letalfen* | — (see note) |

### 3.3 First consonant a guttural (פ' גרונית) and *'* (פ"א)

Gutturals (א, ה, ח, ע) cannot take a silent shva in the pointed system, so the vowels around them change in writing; in speech, א and ע are usually silent or a glottal stop, ה is often dropped, and ח is *kh*. The audible effect in Modern Hebrew is mostly in the vowels *a* / *e* after the prefix.

Notes.
- **Stress distinguishes past from present** in the FS: past קָמָה *káma* "she got up" (penultimate) vs present קָמָה *kama* "she gets up" (final). Likewise *bá'a* / *ba'a*, *shára* / *shara*. In unpointed text they are identical.
- **Hif'il of hollow roots** has *e* in the past and *a* in the future: *hekim* / *yakim*; *hevi* / *yavi*; *hevin* "understood" / *yavin*. Older literary 1SG past forms with *-óti* (הֲקִימוֹתִי *hakimóti*, הֲבִיאוֹתִי) are now archaic.
- **Pi'el and hitpa'el** of hollow roots have two shapes: the old *polel* / *hitpolel*, which repeats the last consonant (קוֹמֵם *komem* "raised up", הִתְקוֹמֵם *hitkomem* "rebelled", הִתְבּוֹנֵן *hitbonen* "observed"), and a newer regular shape with the middle *y* as a consonant (קִיֵּם *kiyem* "kept, fulfilled", הִתְקַיֵּם *hitkayem* "took place", צִיֵּר *tsiyer* "drew", הִתְחַיֵּב *hitkhayev* "committed oneself"). New coinages use the regular shape.
- **Hollow nif'al** is rare (נָסוֹג, נָבוֹךְ, נָכוֹן as adjective); its normative 1SG past *nesogóti* is replaced in speech by *nasógti*. This row is given from normative grammar; UniMorph does not have it.
- **Spoken:** future 1SG *ani yakum*, *ani yavo*, *ani yavi* (§ 10.1 of 05-verbs.md); imperative of הביא is the future *tavi* (*tavi li et ze* "give me that").

### 3.5 Final weak (ל"ה / ל"י)

- The copula היה belongs here: *haya*, *hayíti*, *hayta*; future *yihye*; inf *lihyot*.
- Common verbs of this class: ראה *ra'a* "see" (fut. *yir'e*, inf. *lir'ot*), עשה *asa* "do" (fut. *ya'ase*, inf. *la'asot*), רצה *ratsa* "want", שתה *shata* "drink", בנה *bana* "build", עלה *ala* "go up", קרה *kara* "happen", ניסה *nisa* "try", גילה *gila* "discover", הראה *her'a* "show", התראה *hitra'a* "see each other".

### 3.6 Final *'* (ל"א)

The final א is silent in Modern Hebrew. In pointed writing these verbs differ from final-weak verbs (the stem keeps the *a* or *e* of the strong pattern and the spelling keeps א), but in pronunciation many forms coincide, and speakers often inflect them like ל"ה (05-verbs.md § 10.4).
