# Targum texts, annotated

Verses of Onkelos (Torah) and Jonathan (Prophets), each beside its Hebrew. Texts: Hebrew from the WLC ("Tanach with Nikkud"); Onkelos from Sefaria's public-domain "Onkelos Genesis" … files; Jonathan from Sefaria's public-domain "Mikraot Gedolot" files; all quoted as printed (the printed pointing is late Tiberian, [phonology-orthography.md](phonology-orthography.md) §2). Glossing abbreviations: [../arc/README.md](../arc/README.md). English renderings are our own.

## 1. Creation: Genesis 1:1–5

```
Hebrew:    בְּרֵאשִׁית בָּרָא אֱלֹהִים אֵת הַשָּׁמַיִם וְאֵת הָאָרֶץ
Aramaic:   בְּקַדְמִין בְּרָא יְיָ יָת שְׁמַיָּא וְיָת אַרְעָא
Translit.: bə-qaḏmīn bərā yəyā yāṯ šəmayyā wə-yāṯ ʾarʿā
Gloss:     in-beginning.PL create.PEAL.PF.3MS LORD ACC heaven.DET and-ACC earth.DET
English:   In the beginning the LORD created the heavens and the earth.
Source:    Onkelos Gen 1:1 (public domain)
```

Notes: אֱלֹהִים becomes יְיָ (the God of Israel); the article becomes the determined state; אֵת becomes יָת; בְּרֵאשִׁית becomes בְּקַדְמִין "at first" (a plural of קַדְמִי "first").

```
Hebrew:    וְהָאָרֶץ הָיְתָה תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ וְחֹשֶׁךְ עַל־פְּנֵי תְהוֹם וְרוּחַ אֱלֹהִים מְרַחֶפֶת עַל־פְּנֵי הַמָּיִם
Aramaic:   וְאַרְעָא הֲוַת צָדְיָא וְרֵיקַנְיָא וַחֲשׁוֹכָא פָּרַשׂ עַל אַפֵּי תְהוֹמָא וְרוּחָא מִן קֳדָם יְיָ מְנַשְּׁבָא עַל אַפֵּי מַיָּא
Translit.: wə-ʾarʿā hăwaṯ ṣāḏyā wə-rēqanyā wa-ḥăšōḵā pāraś ʿal ʾappē ṯəhōmā wə-rūḥā min qŏḏām yəyā mənaššəḇā ʿal ʾappē mayyā
Gloss:     and-earth.DET be.PEAL.PF.3FS desolate.F.DET and-empty.F.DET and-darkness.DET spread.PEAL.PTCP.ACT.MS on face.PL.CST deep.DET and-wind.DET from before LORD blow.PAEL.PTCP.ACT.FS on face.PL.CST water.DET
English:   The earth was desolate and empty, darkness was spread over the face of the deep, and a wind from before the LORD was blowing over the face of the waters.
Source:    Onkelos Gen 1:2 (public domain)
```

Notes: the obscure תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ is explained (צָדְיָא וְרֵיקַנְיָא "desolate and empty"); "darkness" gets a verb (פָּרַשׂ "spread"); רוּחַ אֱלֹהִים "the spirit of God" becomes "a wind from before the LORD" — reverence and a naturalistic reading at once ([translation-syntax.md](translation-syntax.md) §3.1).

```
Hebrew:    וַיֹּאמֶר אֱלֹהִים יְהִי אוֹר וַיְהִי־אוֹר
Aramaic:   וַאֲמַר יְיָ יְהֵי נְהוֹרָא וַהֲוָה נְהוֹרָא
Translit.: wa-ʾămar yəyā yəhē nəhōrā wa-hăwā nəhōrā
Gloss:     and-say.PEAL.PF.3MS LORD be.PEAL.IPF.3MS light.DET and-be.PEAL.PF.3MS light.DET
English:   And the LORD said, "Let there be light", and there was light.
Source:    Onkelos Gen 1:3 (public domain)
```

```
Hebrew:    וַיַּרְא אֱלֹהִים אֶת־הָאוֹר כִּי־טוֹב וַיַּבְדֵּל אֱלֹהִים בֵּין הָאוֹר וּבֵין הַחֹשֶׁךְ
Aramaic:   וַחֲזָא יְיָ יָת נְהוֹרָא אֲרֵי טָב וְאַפְרֵשׁ יְיָ בֵּין נְהוֹרָא וּבֵין חֲשׁוֹכָא
Translit.: wa-ḥăzā yəyā yāṯ nəhōrā ʾărē ṭāḇ wə-ʾap̄rēš yəyā bēn nəhōrā ū-ḇēn ḥăšōḵā
Gloss:     and-see.PEAL.PF.3MS LORD ACC light.DET that good and-separate.APH.PF.3MS LORD between light.DET and-between darkness.DET
English:   The LORD saw the light, that it was good, and the LORD separated the light from the darkness.
Source:    Onkelos Gen 1:4 (public domain)
```

Notes: "God saw" is kept: seeing as knowing is not an anthropomorphism the targum avoids. אֲרֵי = כִּי. אַפְרֵשׁ is an aphel (Hebrew hiphil).

```
Hebrew:    וַיִּקְרָא אֱלֹהִים לָאוֹר יוֹם וְלַחֹשֶׁךְ קָרָא לָיְלָה וַיְהִי־עֶרֶב וַיְהִי־בֹקֶר יוֹם אֶחָד
Aramaic:   וּקְרָא יְיָ לִנְהוֹרָא יְמָמָא וְלַחֲשׁוֹכָא קְרָא לֵילְיָא וַהֲוָה רְמַשׁ וַהֲוָה צְפַר יוֹמָא חָד
Translit.: ū-qərā yəyā li-nhōrā yəmāmā wə-la-ḥăšōḵā qərā lēlyā wa-hăwā rəmaš wa-hăwā ṣəp̄ar yōmā ḥāḏ
Gloss:     and-call.PEAL.PF.3MS LORD to-light.DET day(time) and-to-darkness.DET call.PEAL.PF.3MS night and-be.PF.3MS evening and-be.PF.3MS morning day.DET one
English:   The LORD called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. There was evening and there was morning, day one.
Source:    Onkelos Gen 1:5 (public domain)
```

Notes: two different Aramaic words for Hebrew יוֹם: יְמָמָא "daytime" against יוֹמָא "day (of 24 hours)" — the targum disambiguates.

## 2. The first humans: Genesis 2:7, 3:8–9

```
Hebrew:    וַיִּפַּח בְּאַפָּיו נִשְׁמַת חַיִּים וַיְהִי הָאָדָם לְנֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה
Aramaic:   וּנְפַח בְּאַפּוֹהִי נִשְׁמְתָא דְחַיֵּי וַהֲוַת בְּאָדָם לְרוּחַ מְמַלְלָא
Translit.: ū-nəp̄aḥ bə-ʾappōhī nišməṯā ḏə-ḥayyē wa-hăwaṯ bə-ʾāḏām lə-rūaḥ məmallā
Gloss:     and-blow.PEAL.PF.3MS in-nostril.PL-3MS breath.DET REL-life.PL and-be.PEAL.PF.3FS in-Adam to-spirit speak.PAEL.PTCP.ACT.FS
English:   He blew into his nostrils the breath of life, and it became in man a speaking spirit.
Source:    Onkelos Gen 2:7b (public domain)
```

Notes: a construct (נִשְׁמַת חַיִּים) becomes a ד־ phrase; "a living soul" becomes "a speaking spirit": what makes man human is speech.

```
Hebrew:    וַיִּשְׁמְעוּ אֶת־קוֹל יְהוָה אֱלֹהִים מִתְהַלֵּךְ בַּגָּן לְרוּחַ הַיּוֹם
Aramaic:   וּשְׁמָעוּ יָת קַל מֵימְרָא דַּיְיָ אֱלֹהִים מְהַלֵּךְ בְּגִינְתָא לִמְנַח יוֹמָא
Translit.: ū-šəmāʿū yāṯ qal mēmrā da-yəyā ʾĕlōhīm məhallēḵ bə-ḡīntā li-mnaḥ yōmā
Gloss:     and-hear.PEAL.PF.3MPL ACC voice.CST Memra.DET REL-LORD God walk.PAEL.PTCP.ACT.MS in-garden.DET to-rest.INF.CST day.DET
English:   They heard the voice of the Memra of the LORD God going about in the garden at the resting of the day.
Source:    Onkelos Gen 3:8a (public domain)
```

```
Hebrew:    וַיִּקְרָא יְהוָה אֱלֹהִים אֶל־הָאָדָם וַיֹּאמֶר לוֹ אַיֶּכָּה
Aramaic:   וּקְרָא יְיָ אֱלֹהִים לְאָדָם וַאֲמַר לֵהּ אָן אָתְּ
Translit.: ū-qərā yəyā ʾĕlōhīm lə-ʾāḏām wa-ʾămar lēh ʾān ʾāt
Gloss:     and-call.PEAL.PF.3MS LORD God to-Adam and-say.PEAL.PF.3MS to-3MS where you
English:   The LORD God called to Adam and said to him, "Where are you?"
Source:    Onkelos Gen 3:9 (public domain)
```

Notes: the Hebrew one-word question אַיֶּכָּה is split into its parts.

## 3. Cain and Abel: Genesis 4:8

```
Hebrew:    וַיֹּאמֶר קַיִן אֶל־הֶבֶל אָחִיו וַיְהִי בִּהְיוֹתָם בַּשָּׂדֶה וַיָּקָם קַיִן אֶל־הֶבֶל אָחִיו וַיַּהַרְגֵהוּ
Aramaic:   וַאֲמַר קַיִן לְהֶבֶל אַחוֹהִי וַהֲוָה בְּמֶהֱוֵיהוֹן בְּחַקְלָא וְקָם קַיִן עַל הֶבֶל אַחוֹהִי וְקַטְלֵהּ
Translit.: wa-ʾămar qayin lə-heḇel ʾaḥōhī wa-hăwā bə-mehĕwēhōn bə-ḥaqlā wə-qām qayin ʿal heḇel ʾaḥōhī wə-qaṭlēh
Gloss:     and-say.PF.3MS Cain to-Abel brother-3MS and-be.PF.3MS in-be.INF-3MPL in-field.DET and-rise.PF.3MS Cain against Abel brother-3MS and-kill.PEAL.PF.3MS-3MS
English:   Cain spoke to Abel his brother; and when they were in the field, Cain rose against Abel his brother and killed him.
Source:    Onkelos Gen 4:8 (public domain)
```

Notes: Onkelos leaves the gap of the Hebrew ("Cain said to Abel …" — said what?) as it is. The Palestinian targums and Pseudo-Jonathan fill it with a whole dispute between the brothers ([../jpa/texts.md](../jpa/texts.md)): the clearest single contrast between the two targum traditions.

## 5. Jacob's vow: Genesis 28:20–21

```
Hebrew:    אִם־יִהְיֶה אֱלֹהִים עִמָּדִי וּשְׁמָרַנִי בַּדֶּרֶךְ הַזֶּה אֲשֶׁר אָנֹכִי הוֹלֵךְ
```
