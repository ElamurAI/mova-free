# Measures and counted facts

**Gist.** Some numbers a problem never says because everyone knows them: an hour has 60 minutes, a metre 100
centimetres, a quarter is 25 cents, a triangle has 3 sides, a cube 6 faces. A word problem that names the word and
asks in the unit leans on them: «2 hours … how many minutes» is 2 × 60.

`global::measure(word)` gives (how many, of what) for each. The small one adds such a fact to the told quantities only
when the text names both the word and the unit (or the unit's plural).
