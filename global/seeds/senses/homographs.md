# Words written alike, meaning different things

Each sense is a concept with its code — the language, the word, the sense (`en:fly:insect`, `en:fly:move`). Its words
are the word itself and the words around it that tell the sense: `global::sense_of("en", "fly", text)` picks the
sense whose cues the text has most of (on a tie, the first written here — the commoner one). The small one met this
in its knowledge: «people fly» was read as the insect.

## Compounds

A word made of several, joined by «_»: the sense is the compound's, not its parts'.

```concept
en:give_up:quit: give_up gave_up quit stop trying
en:give_up:hand_over: give_up hand_over surrender yield
en:pick_up:lift: pick_up picked_up lift take floor
en:pick_up:collect: pick_up picked_up collect fetch school car
en:run_out: run_out ran_out none left empty
en:take_away: take_away took_away subtract remove minus
en:add_up: add_up added_up total sum
en:put_away: put_away store shelf box
```
