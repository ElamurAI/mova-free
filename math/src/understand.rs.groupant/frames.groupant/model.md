## models

## behavior_complement

Unknown alphabetic token cannot reach frame dispatch. A bare Fahrenheit target cannot supply a source scale for20 degrees. Later frame triggers cannot override an earlier selected frame, even if the earlier frame fails.

## reverse_reconstruction

The same Expr may originate from a direct quantity, a sole condition quantity, or different synonym-based function frames. An unknown-word error admits sentences with or without numbers because that gate runs first.

## condition_selection

All preceding sentence items are conditions; in the last sentence only cond=true items qualify. A lone condition quantity can supply source; multiple earlier-sentence quantities can fail as ambiguous.

## scale_transport

A Scale quantity becomes Temp unless delta=true, when it becomes Qty with the delta unit. Degrees uses default_scale or fails; it does not infer source scale from conversion destination.
