# Source-scoped typed relations

## behavior_complement

A composite unresolved by Pollard excludes complete=true, but Probable factor does not. Negative n excludes negative factor entries because magnitude is factored.

## reverse_reconstruction

Nonempty factors reconstruct |n| as product p^e, losing sign. Empty producer factors admit n=0,1,-1. Sorted merged factors lose split/trial traversal history and time/budget usage.

## primality_classes

u64 uses12 fixed prime bases. Larger inputs try small primes,30 fixed bases, depth-limited Pocklington, then Probable. Fixed bases do not justify a randomized4^-30 error statement.

## pocklington_condition

Accumulate only proven prime factors of n-1 into F; require F²>n and for each q a<200 with a^(n-1)=1 and gcd(a^((n-1)/q)-1,n)=1. Failure to find witnesses returns no certificate.

## budget_effect

Pollard decrements budget in blocks; trial/primality, initial doubling and recovery omit the check. u64 ignores deadline; big checks deadline only within blocks. A budget is not total arithmetic work.

## factor_certificate

check_factor multiplies powers and requires complete plus prime-like factors; zero with empty list is specially accepted. It repeats probable acceptance and cannot convert that status to proven prime.
