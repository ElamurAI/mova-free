## models

# Source-scoped typed relations

## behavior_complement

phi/tau/sigma/mobius reject nonpositive n; divisors first takes magnitude and rejects zero but accepts negative values. digits base<2 rejects, from_digits imposes no such gate.

## reverse_reconstruction

digits(n,b) loses sign: n and -n share output. Canonical digits and known base recover abs(n); from_digits with arbitrary coefficients admits carrying transformations and leading zeros.

## multiplicative_formula

Given complete factors, phi=product p^(e-1)(p-1), tau=product(e+1), sigma_k=product sum(j=0..e)p^(kj), mobius=0 if repeated factor else(-1)^count.

## divisor_materialization

Count product(e+1) in u128 is checked against max before allocation, then products are enumerated and sorted. Count arithmetic itself is unchecked; complete probable factors remain accepted.

## prime_search

prime_pi caps n at2e8; nth_prime k in1..1e7 chooses analytic sieve bound and indexes k-1. next_prime/prev_prime step one integer at a time and accept prime-like values, with no iteration cap.

## zero_digits

digits0=[0], including nondecimal base; from_digits([],b)=0 for any b. Decimal path converts magnitude text, others repeatedly divide and reverse remainders.
