## models

# Source-scoped typed relations

## behavior_complement

check_solution rejects A.len!=b.len, then any row.len!=x.len, before arithmetic; a malformed shape cannot pass by truncated zip. Matching shapes with any dot product !=b_i also reject.

## reverse_reconstruction

Accepted x constrains b=A*x for every shape-valid A, so many systems share it. Unique additionally requires full column rank. Infinite particular/free does not encode a nullspace basis or recover A.

## pivot_selection

gauss chooses first nonzero row at/after current row for each column, normalizes entire augmented row and eliminates pivot from all other rows. Exact arithmetic needs no epsilon tie policy.

## rank_classification

Any remaining reduced row0=c!=0 gives Inconsistent; otherwise pivot coordinates take RHS and all free coordinates remain0. Pivots count n means Unique, fewer gives Infinite with ascending free indices.

## shape_precedence

Checker count mismatch is O(1) and precedes O(m) width scan. Empty A,b accepts arbitrary x. gauss retains its own unchecked rectangular/matched precondition and is not made total by checker repair.

## arithmetic_cost

After shape validation substitution evaluates m length-n rational dot products O(mn) plus mismatch text. Gauss is O(m*n*min(m,n)) rational operations/O(mn) storage; bit growth is additional.
