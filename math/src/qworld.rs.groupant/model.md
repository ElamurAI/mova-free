## behavior_complement

Rates exclude immediate cell-map updates but can change inherited owner/thing context for later commands; constants exclude commands; zero denominator excludes ratio success. Negative states remain possible; Diff fallback needs >=2 cmds even if target count>=2.

## reverse_reconstruction

Final overwrite hides earlier state; command histories with same later net change are compatible. Script labels lose ignored lines and duplicate-valued antecedents.

## overwrite_law

Set(v1);Set(v2) leaves v2, unlike qread's additive Set.
