## invariant-and-cost

# Invariant and cost

## Invariant

Flags refer to computed chains; score is a fixed heuristic in 2..4, empty flags produce lens score0. Input case and jurisdiction are cloned or borrowed unchanged.

## Cost

Let L be lattice cost, T tipping cost, P points, D enabled devices, S present status pairs<=6. flags costs O((1+2D+S)L+T+P*E+F log F) plus strings; E is evaluate cost and F emitted flags.

Symbolic bounds, not timings. Resource exhaustion is not tested.
