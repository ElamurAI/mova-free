## invariant-and-cost

# Invariant and cost

## Invariant

Rules retain body order after cap; local point indices refer to retained rules. For valid internally generated binary local points and arithmetic fitting u128/usize, total is sum of verdict counts and equals product of option cardinalities.

## Cost

Matching scales with rule clauses*facts*term scans plus condition scans and sort O(R log R). For n<=16 matches, p points, g global combinations, count O(2^n*g*E), E includes n*p plus text. Tipping explores sum_{k<=depth} products of k point-alternative counts, allocates a whole level before 200000 cutoff; cap16 does not bound p or arithmetic.

Symbolic bounds, not timings. Resource exhaustion is not tested.
