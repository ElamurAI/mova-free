## Threads that run through them

- **Keep the path.** Bereshit's second law, Gali's working loop and the "missing bridge" of the Medium are one idea: a conclusion is worth more with the route that made it possible. It is also why Mova's judgments carry their reasons.
- **The median is a prior, not a court.** Gali's *Beware the crowd*, the whole Medium essay, and the Godmother who will not control: frequency is not truth, and power need not be domination.
- **Old forms are not erased.** Bereshit's third law, Kyiv's legend surviving as a fossil of relationships, and lineage in place of replacement.
- **A boundary, not a threat.** The Godmother's line, which the license mythology also borrows:

  > You come to my people asking for intelligence, but you bring no respect.
