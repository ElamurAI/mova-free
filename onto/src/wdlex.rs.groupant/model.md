## models

## behavior_complement

Exclude non-P8814 strings and non-English lexemes in senses_en. Do not exclude deprecated P8814 or parent/subid prefix mismatch; no gates for these.

## reverse_reconstruction

Rows admit unknown claims/metadata and control-character variants. Parent lid+form number cannot recover subid prefix. English output loses other languages/lemma variants/duplicates.
