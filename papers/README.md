# Papers Mova uses

The scientific works Mova is built on, one row each, linked to its card on Elamur Research (a summary in our own words, the link, authors, the license of the paper, how Mova uses it, and how much it helped). The cards live in the Research library, not in this repo. The «Effect» column: **measured** marks a card that reports a number from Mova's own experiments, including ideas that did not help; the others describe the role of the work (a rule source, a design, background) and say it was not measured separately. What came of each measured work, and the day its numbers were first recorded in the repo, is in [measured.tsv](measured.tsv); the site gathers them, with each card's numbers, on one page by pipeline stage. The «Stage» column is the stage of Mova's pipeline the work serves (tokens, tree, frames, world, answer, learn, back), and the «Role» column what it is to that stage: **implements** — the code stands on its method, or tried it (a measured idea with no gain stays here, marked so); **measured** — Mova is measured against it (a benchmark, a metric, a scoring convention, a reference result); **rules** — a source of hand-written rules or knowledge (the old grammars among them); **background** — reading behind a decision. The role follows `measured.tsv` (a metric or our score on a benchmark is «measured»), the usage log (`docs/usage.tsv` and what `papers usage` finds: a rule source) and the card's «How Mova uses it»; the site's pipeline page groups each stage's works by it.

The papers themselves are not copied here; follow the links.

**Every card, its authors, topics and who cites it, live on Elamur Research: [research.elamur.ai/free/projects/mova/](https://research.elamur.ai/free/projects/mova/)** — the papers Mova applies and how. This page keeps the short list of the works Mova is built on.
