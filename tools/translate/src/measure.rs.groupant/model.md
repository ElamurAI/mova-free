### translation-flow-measure

reader_res converts MTok to nodes, prealigns lowercase lemmas by LCS plus equal-length gaps, encodes tree script and charges moved positions outside a strict LIS. reader_block concatenates verses with § sentinel and shifted nonzero heads, checks script apply and order_ok. Text encoding records noncanonical literal gaps and tail. Run::verse measures normal output separately, builds reader/encoder/stack residual, decodes with first-failure precedence meta-world→meta order→tree→tokens→text; direct mode recurses once with Words.

## invariant-and-cost

### Domain, invariant and cost

#### Invariant

Trees have valid heads and aligned forms/gaps; base is permutation1..n, moved sorted distinct subset. choose_bits requires0<=k<=n; offsets/counts fit usize/i64. Finite residual arithmetic. Exact gate concerns target reproduction using residual, not residual-free translation correctness or learning.

#### Cost

base_order O(nlog n), moved O(nlog n) time/O(n) memory, order_ok O(nlog k); prealign delegates LCS O(nm) time/state. Reader scripts delegate matching costs. Text encode/decode O(tokens+literal bytes) plus ordered map insertion/lookup. Direct recursion adds one Words pass, never unbounded recursion.

Integer additions/casts require representability; finite floating inputs where comparisons are modeled. These symbolic costs are not measurements of corpus throughput. No useful cheaper executable percentage surrogate established; future supported caps only 1/2/5/10%.
