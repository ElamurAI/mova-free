#### Cost

Let S be sentence vectors, W total words, B cloned string bytes, and A anchor/link/role references visited. par_map uses O(n) result slots, O(max(t,1)) thread startup and the sum of callback work; mutex assignment is serialized. join_trees costs O(S+W+B), including iteration over empty sentence vectors. anchored costs O(W+B+A*(W+1)): initial word/string cloning occurs even when A=0, and guarded head walks contribute the reference-dependent term. parse_book retains frames/world per discourse; actual annotation costs are delegated and unexecuted.

Integer additions/casts require representability; finite floating inputs where comparisons are modeled. These symbolic costs are not measurements of corpus throughput. No useful cheaper executable percentage surrogate established; future supported caps only 1/2/5/10%.
