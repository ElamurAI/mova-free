## invariant-and-cost

### Question coordinates and anchor list parser invariants and costs

#### Invariant

Returned anchors nonempty but can repeat. para may be negative; k positive. Empty answer and unknown qtype remain admitted until database validation.

#### Cost

O(file bytes + anchor bytes), materializes all rows and anchors.

Symbolic bounds are source-derived, not measured wall-clock speed or universal complexity proof. Allocation failures, integer exhaustion and concurrency are outside finite evidence.
