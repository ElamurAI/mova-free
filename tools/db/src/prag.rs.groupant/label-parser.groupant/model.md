## invariant-and-cost

### Eight or nine label columns invariants and costs

#### Invariant

Every returned row has8 label columns plus separate sent_id. Parser does not validate bits, duplicate IDs or closed labels.

#### Cost

O(file bytes) time and retained row memory; header and per-line split allocate column vectors.

Symbolic bounds are source-derived, not measured wall-clock speed or universal complexity proof. Allocation failures, integer exhaustion and concurrency are outside finite evidence.
