## invariant-and-cost

### Invariant and cost

#### Invariant

All rows are license-validated before download setup. Existing wildcard choice is filesystem-order dependent and bypasses signature/STOP/cap checks. Done item_idx must be in range. Aggregate format uses HashSet iteration; do not claim byte-deterministic mixed-format JSON.

#### Cost

List O(B); hashing O(file bytes) with 1 MiB buffer. Archive filtering O(uncompressed bytes) plus gzip cost; max kept entry 5 MiB but total input has no cap. Manifest O(D log S) plus serialized bytes; effects and delays are external costs.
