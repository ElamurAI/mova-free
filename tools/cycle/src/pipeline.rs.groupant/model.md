## training_status_partition

Domain: one collect invocation reaching its final report under fixed configuration/artifact observations, trusted configured subprocess semantics, successful allocations and writable reporting. Let E be the result of finetune_rounds(), M mean a nonempty configured gparse model, and C mean the round candidate model.safetensors exists when that gate is reached. The first applicable branch defines status: not E gives disabled; E and not M gives no_configured_model; E and M and C gives existing_candidate; E and M and not C gives completed_here only after training command success and candidate-directory rename success. Failure before the final report emits no successful round summary. These are categorical observations, not approximate percentages or a claim about learner quality.

Exclusion: disabled/missing-model/existing-candidate branches cannot truthfully report a completed training invocation. A true E alone does not imply training, and the report does not establish GPU use. Counterexample to the old wording: E=false with otherwise successful collection skips the train command but formerly printed GPU SLM fine-tuned. Evidence: direct source-control-flow review and focused offline/locked compilation; no collect, DB, teacher or trainer invocation is exercised for this reporting fix. Frozen old audit logs remain historical evidence.

## training_status_preimage

Observation: the final round summary's training suffix, keeping the preexisting numeric metrics separate. Its compatible input/effect preimages are disabled -> not E with M/C unobserved; no_configured_model -> E and not M with C unobserved; existing_candidate -> E and M and C without new training; completed_here -> E and M and not C followed by successful train-command return, rename and later row write. The configured executable is trusted to implement its declared command; neither command success nor this suffix proves a quality improvement, actual GPU execution, candidate integrity or any unique earlier history.

Counterexample: disabled training and a missing configured model can coexist, but disabled wins because the guards retain their original short-circuit order. An existing candidate does not reconstruct who trained it or when. Evidence status: partial source-derived model, compile checked only; live effects and historical log reinterpretation remain outside this change.

## invariant-and-cost

### Invariant and cost

Current CLI admits Codex and rejects tiers for new training, while legacy branches remain modeled but unexecuted. No atomic collect transaction. Zero inflight can stall; to+1 and seed/index arithmetic assume no overflow. Reservation appends/syncs before invocation; failures may consume budget.

Cascade B input bytes, N blocks and maximum id length L_id: O(B+N log(N+1)*(1+L_id)+N min(n,N)), including sort_by_key's repeated id hashing and linear contains scans; output O(selected bytes). Scheduler per iteration scans flights/reviews/markers plus unbounded external effects and sleeps. Reservation O(ledger bytes) read plus append/sync. No throughput claim.

No executable percentage surrogate is claimed: effects, categorical acceptance and report text have no justified approximate metric here. Future profiles require a useful metric, zero case, reference and evidence at caps 1/2/5/10%.

The final training report is invocation-specific. Branch priority is disabled, missing configured model, existing candidate, then actual successful training-command/publication prefix. No completion claim follows merely from an enabled flag. This adds only constant-size status selection to the existing guards; external work, training admission, apply_metrics and row numbers are unchanged. Candidate presence is an existence gate, not an integrity check or proof of new training.
