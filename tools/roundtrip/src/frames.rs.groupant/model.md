## invariant-and-cost

### Invariant and cost

Realization never reads Ent.text as surface oracle. A damaged script with absent Take origin yields an empty Dep placeholder, not successful recovery of unavailable source text. Normal realization does not imply semantic equivalence.

Given realized tree r and encoder script s, apply(r,s) is a reconstruction witness for original parsed tree. Fixed output alone admits many frame/link/TAM states and discarded realizer nodes; scripts with missing or duplicate Take origins need not identify unique antecedents.

With p parsed and r realized nodes and B lemma bytes, greedy matching O(p*r*B); two structural passes same pair bound. apply O(r+p+payload bytes) memory/time excluding deep clones. Realizer scans all F frames per clause O(F^2) plus recursive entity expansion, membership logs, punctuation cost and payload clones; recursive depth is unbounded.
