Meta does not contribute total_bits, per(x,0) is zero, row exact_ok=2 is false. Errors from jobs cannot count as exact successes. Outputs are not transactional: later write failure can leave earlier files.

Rows erase Tally examples, most counters and provenance; compatible tallies share projected fields and exact/damaged predicates. Rounded tables admit intervals of f64 values and cannot uniquely recover cells. Earlier files alone do not establish successful final report.

#### Cost

row O(cells), lbits O(cells), total_bits four cell scans. median O(B log B) time/O(B) owned buffer. Reporting loops corpora×layers×classes and sorts cell tables; orchestration costs pipeline jobs plus retained rows and serialized output bytes.

Integer additions/products and allocation sizes must fit native types. Floating costs require finite values; no throughput or all-input guarantee follows from synthetic tests.
