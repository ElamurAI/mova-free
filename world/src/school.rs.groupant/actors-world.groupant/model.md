## forward

Decode vote duplicates in i128, clamp each final merged total once to i64. effect sums each marginal in i128, omits NONE=9, selects greatest vote then smallest signed effect; missing receiver defaults zero, missing subject yields None. Supplied Reading play initializes holdings and executes events in order, fractions from previous subject balance minus keep. answer_of chooses its first matching schema; focus filters by thing.

## invariant

Duplicate order cannot change a representable total; saturation happens only after summation. Independent marginals may select a pair never voted jointly. play/answer require nonzero fraction denominators and representable arithmetic. Reader globals/dictionary/fallback are external.

## cost

Decode O(R log V log E), O(VE) storage; marginal election O(E log E). play O((H+J)log A+JH) due to fallback-holder scans; answer up to O(J+A). Parsing/guessing opaque.

## wave34-event-play

Current nested child world-school-actors-world-event-play: Fold all initial holdings by actor. For each ordered event obtain (-1,0) for pay, else eff(verb) or return None.
