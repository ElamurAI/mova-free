# world-school-student-state-selection

## forward

Choose T=acc when nonempty, else w. Sum T[f,o] over unique features in i128 and maximize (score,-op discriminant) over allowed; empty allowed defaults Add. Positive reasons for winner sort by decreasing weight then lexical key, truncated to three. Answer applies the chosen op.

## invariant

Tie order is Add<Sub<Mul<Div, regardless of allowed order. Why excludes nonpositive weights. On the supported 64-bit target at most usize::MAX i64 contributions fit in i128. p.op does not affect selection.

## cost

After feature generation O(F log K) scoring over constant four operations, O(F log F) reason sorting and O(F) storage. Scalar widening keeps this bound; constants unmeasured.

## composition

The same table feeds score and reasons. Tests compare MAX+MAX to MAX-1, reversed allowed order, empty allowed, and an unrelated accumulator activating the averaged table. No parser or teacher.
