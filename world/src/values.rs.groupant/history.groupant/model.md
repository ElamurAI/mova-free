# value-state-history

## Forward

Record goal before achievement and scan goal peers lexically; then the set-transition child resolves external-character harm/help with frozen prior-history eligibility.

## Invariant

Unique harm agents per target and at most one helped pair persist throughout invocation.

## Cost

O(L) stored histories; achievement can scan O(L) peers with O(L) rest/rival histories, O(L^2) per achievement worst-case.

## Composition

Achievement precedes external-character guards; self cause may achieve but cannot harm/help. Rule lookup gates emissions while history updates can survive missing rules. The child owns external-state transitions; goal timing and self-achievement stay here. These overlapping scopes do not add independent costs.
