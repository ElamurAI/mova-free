## reverse_reconstruction

Empty carry admits no-object, dropped and transferred-away histories. holder[o]=b admits any history ending with effective take(b,o) and no later effective drop. Overwritten locations are unrecoverable. Formally Pre(H*)={h | dom(final_holder(h))=dom(H*) and ∀o∈dom(H*), last_surviving_take(h,o)=H*[o]}. Thus every absent target key is absent in the final holder map; for H*=empty all earlier acquisitions must have been effectively dropped. Arbitrary overwritten intermediate transfers remain compatible.
