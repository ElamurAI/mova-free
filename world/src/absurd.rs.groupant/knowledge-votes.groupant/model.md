# world-absurd-knowledge-votes

## forward

learn_cells filters lowercase last subject and first object tokens longer than two bytes on Can/Does facts. Negative unconditional facts vote subject 4, negative unconditioned-object facts vote subject 3, negative conditionals abstain. Positive facts vote subject 0 or Rarely 1 and final nonconditional object 0. Highest count wins with lower-score tie break; each score retains its first source.

## invariant

Negated facts never generate object votes. learn_args omits a whole cell when either role has a rated counterpart. Fact provenance and lexical heuristics do not establish physical truth. This fragment is source inspected, not natively exercised.
