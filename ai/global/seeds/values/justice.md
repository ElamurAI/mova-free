# Justice

**Gist.** Do not take what is not yours; give each their due; greed ruins.

**Conditions and exceptions.** Taking your own is not theft.

**Examples.**
- The Fox and the Crow: the fox took someone else's cheese by cunning.
- The Dog and Its Reflection (Aesop): the dog wanted the other piece in the water too and lost its own. Greed ruins.

**Sources.** Aesop's fables; "you shall not steal" (Ex 20:15).
