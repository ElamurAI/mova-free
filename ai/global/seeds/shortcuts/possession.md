# Possession and changes in quantity

**Gist.** The verb says how the owner's quantity changes (ARIS, Hosseini 2014; Roy & Roth 2018: HAVE, GET, GIVE, CONSTRUCT, DESTROY).

**Conditions and exceptions.** "Give" decreases for the giver and increases for the receiver; which of the two is asked is decided by the question.

**Examples.** "She eats three" — she has three fewer; "He buys 5 more" — five more.

**Sources.** Hosseini et al. 2014 (EMNLP); Roy & Roth 2018 (TACL).

```link
get -> increase : got — the owner has more
give -> decrease : gave — the giver has less
give -> increase : gave — the receiver has more
make -> increase : made — there is more
lose -> decrease : lost, ate, spent — there is less
```

```concept
increase: increase more gain
decrease: decrease less fewer left remain
```
