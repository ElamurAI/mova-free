### Cost

Ordinary closed scanning O(B); repeated missing closer searches O(B²). Returned metadata O(F+U), bodies borrowed. i32 depth unchecked overflow on unbounded input.

Allocation failure, stack exhaustion and unchecked counter overflow have no additional certificate. No timing claim or global error bound.

## models

## behavior_complement

Escaped dollar cannot open; positive brace depth excludes inline close; blank lines stop inline search. Bracket/environment matching is not nested TeX parsing. Unclosed verbatim may resume internal scanning.

## reverse_reconstruction

Compatible documents contain arbitrary ignored text constrained by byte offsets and scanner state. Delimiter metadata constrains spellings but cannot recover prose, comments or preambles. Empty result admits empty/prose/comment-only input.
