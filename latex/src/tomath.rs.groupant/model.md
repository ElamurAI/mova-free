## models

## behavior_complement

Unsupported variables/functions/scripts/constructs categorized, relation precheck preempts malformed operand. No solving/evaluation/definedness guarantee: 1/0 can structurally roundtrip while numeric evaluator rejects. Scripts missing sup can panic via unwrap for manual AST.

## reverse_reconstruction

Expr preimage includes alternative grouping, decimal/fraction forms, fonted Euler e. from_expr chosen presentation, not total inverse. fold_literals maps integer Div(p,q) with p>=0 and q>1 to Rat when Rat construction succeeds and its normalized numerator still equals p; this includes Div(0,2) mapping to zero despite reducibility. Nested operation shape otherwise remains.

## failure_order

Top-level relation before seq; unsupported relation before operands; function arity before emission support.

## literal_policy

Frac/DFrac/TFrac irreducible integers denom>1 become Rat; CFrac stays Div.

## precedence

sin 2x=sin(2*x); sin x cos x product; closing script powers group.

## inverse_domain

Empty Add/Mul emits empty tree rejected by inverse/roundtrip; arbitrary public constructors not guaranteed.
